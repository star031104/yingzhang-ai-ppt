# Synthetic dashboard

All metrics are synthetic and designed for benchmark evaluation.

## Overview

Conversion was 12%, retention was 78%, and satisfaction was 91%. Monthly conversion rose from 9% in January to 11% in February and 12% in March. Day-7 retention was 82%, day-30 retention 78%, and day-90 retention 64%.

## Acquisition and devices

Partner conversion was 16%, search conversion 12%, and social conversion 8%. Mobile conversion reached 10% compared with 15% on desktop; mobile accounts for 68% of visits.

## Experience and data quality

Overall satisfaction reached 91%; response completion was 73% across 620 synthetic survey responses. The dashboard covers 18,400 synthetic sessions; 2.1% of sessions lack a channel label.

## Limits

The sample is synthetic and observational; channel differences do not prove causal impact.
