# Synthetic product launch

All figures are synthetic and for benchmark use only.

## Launch goal

The pilot reached 10,000 synthetic users in 30 days and targeted first-session activation.

## Audience

The sample includes 6,000 individual users and 4,000 team users recruited from three synthetic channels.

## Acquisition

Partner referrals brought 4,200 users, owned content 3,100 users, and events 2,700 users.

## Activation

First-session activation reached 58%; guided setup reached 71% compared with 46% for self-service setup.

## Retention

Day-30 retention was 38% overall, 44% for guided setup, and 31% for self-service setup.

## Feedback

Synthetic satisfaction averaged 4.3 out of 5, while 18% of respondents requested clearer onboarding.

## Constraints

The pilot uses synthetic records, short observation windows, and no randomized control group.

## Next step

Run a controlled onboarding test, define a support capacity limit, and review retention after 90 days.
