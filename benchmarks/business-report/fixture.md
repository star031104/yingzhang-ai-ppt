# Synthetic business report

All figures in this benchmark are synthetic and exist only to exercise evidence-linked business reporting.

## Executive summary

Revenue increased 18% across the sample period, while the average service cycle fell 11%.

## Baseline

The synthetic team handled 1,200 requests in January with a median cycle time of 5.2 days.

## Revenue mix

Subscription revenue was 62% of total revenue, services contributed 25%, and other products contributed 13%.

## Service performance

Median cycle time moved from 5.2 days in January to 4.6 days in March; the team completed 94% of requests within its target.

## Customer segments

Enterprise renewal reached 91%, while small-business renewal was 84%; the sample contains 240 synthetic accounts.

## Operating costs

Support cost per request declined from 42 to 37 synthetic units after workflow consolidation.

## Risks and limits

The sample excludes seasonal demand and uses synthetic records; it does not establish a causal relationship.

## Recommendation

Continue the workflow pilot for one quarter, track service quality alongside cycle time, and reassess staffing before broader rollout.
